## 4. Delivery

4.1 The Supplier shall deliver the ordered quantities on the delivery date stated in each purchase order. Partial deliveries are permitted only when each delivery is accompanied by a delivery note referencing the purchase order and line.

4.2 Where a delivery is late by more than five (5) working days, the Supplier shall notify the Buyer in writing with a revised delivery date.

## 6. Invoicing

6.1 The Supplier may invoice only quantities that have been delivered and accepted by the Buyer at the receiving location. Quantities in transit, back-ordered or not yet accepted shall not be invoiced.

6.2 Each invoice shall reference the purchase order number and line. Invoices that exceed accepted quantities will be held until a credit note or corrected invoice is received.

## 7. Prices

7.1 Unit prices stated in the purchase order are fixed for twelve (12) months from the order date unless amended in writing by both parties.

## 8. Rejected goods

8.1 Goods rejected at incoming inspection remain the Supplier's property. The Supplier shall collect and replace rejected goods within ten (10) working days. Rejected goods shall not be invoiced.
