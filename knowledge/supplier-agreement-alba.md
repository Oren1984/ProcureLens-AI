## 3. Pricing and price changes

3.1 Prices are those agreed in the purchase order.

3.2 The Supplier may request a price adjustment for raw-material cost changes by giving at least sixty (60) days' written notice. A price adjustment takes effect only after the Buyer confirms it in writing and issues an amended purchase order.

3.3 Invoices issued at prices other than the purchase-order price without an amended purchase order shall be returned to the Supplier for correction.

## 5. Freight and ancillary charges

5.1 Deliveries are DAP Buyer's warehouse. Freight is included in the unit price unless the purchase order contains a separate freight line. Separately invoiced freight without a purchase-order line requires prior written approval.

## 9. Electric motors

9.1 Motors are critical maintenance spares. Delivery dates are binding; the Supplier shall report any risk of delay immediately.
