# Procurement Policy — Invoice Verification

## 1. Three-way match

1.1 Supplier invoices for goods are paid only after a three-way match between the purchase order, the goods receipt (accepted quantity) and the invoice.

1.2 Matching is performed at purchase-order line level using the document references, not item descriptions.

## 2. Price tolerance

2.1 An invoice unit price may deviate from the purchase-order price by up to two percent (2%) without further approval. Larger deviations require an amended purchase order or written approval by the category buyer.

## 3. Human decision ownership

3.1 Exception decisions are made by an authorised procurement reviewer. Software tools may prepare evidence and drafts but do not approve payment, change records or contact suppliers on their own.

3.2 Every resolution records the reviewer, the date and the reason.

## 4. Currency and units

4.1 Invoices in a currency other than the company currency, and documents that require unit-of-measure conversion, are reviewed manually by accounts payable.
