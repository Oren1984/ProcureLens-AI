# Partial Delivery Rules

## Accepting partial deliveries

A partial delivery is recorded as its own goods receipt against the purchase-order line. Several receipts may exist for one line; the accepted quantities are added together.

## Invoicing partial deliveries

Suppliers may invoice each partial delivery separately. The total invoiced quantity for a line must not exceed the total accepted quantity. Where it does, the excess is held until the supplier delivers the remainder or issues a credit note.

## Outstanding quantities

The outstanding quantity is the ordered quantity minus the accepted quantity. Rejected goods do not reduce the outstanding quantity. Outstanding quantities past the scheduled date are followed up with the supplier for a confirmed delivery date.

## Returns

Returns to the supplier are processed by the warehouse and accounts payable outside the standard matching workflow.
