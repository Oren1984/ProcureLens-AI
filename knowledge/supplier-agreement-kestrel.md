# Packaging Supply Terms — Kestrel Packaging BV

## 2. Delivery schedule

2.1 Call-off quantities shall be delivered on the scheduled date in the purchase order. Partial deliveries are acceptable when the remaining quantity follows within seven (7) days.

2.2 If delivery is more than ten (10) days late, the Buyer may cancel the undelivered remainder without liability or source it elsewhere.

## 4. Quality and rejection

4.1 Pallets and boxes failing inspection (moisture, damage, missing heat-treatment marking) are rejected. Rejected quantities are not accepted deliveries and may not be invoiced until replacements are delivered and accepted.

## 5. Invoicing

5.1 One invoice per delivery. Invoices must reference the purchase order and the delivery note.
