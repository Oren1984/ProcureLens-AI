# Invoice Discrepancy Handling Procedure

## Step 1 — Verify the facts

Confirm the purchase order, all goods receipts and all invoices for the affected line. Check that no receipt is waiting to be posted and that cancelled documents are excluded.

## Step 2 — Contact the supplier

Send a factual clarification request that states the order, item, quantities or prices concerned and asks for a corrected invoice, a credit note or a confirmed delivery date. Do not make payment commitments in the clarification.

## Step 3 — Decide and record

Resolve the exception only when the discrepancy is explained or corrected. Record the reason, for example "credit note CN-123 received" or "remaining quantity received on 12 October". Reopen the exception if new information contradicts the decision.

## Step 4 — Escalation

Escalate to the category buyer when the supplier disputes the discrepancy, or when the exposure exceeds 5,000 in company currency.
